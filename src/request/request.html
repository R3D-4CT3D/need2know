Ask to delete your data

A fingerprint can't be un-taken, but privacy laws let you make the site delete what it stored with it, stop selling it, and pass the request on to the companies it shared it with. This page writes the letter, with what the extension recorded as evidence. You send it yourself, from your own email or the site's privacy form, so the reply comes to you and the request is legally yours.

      Data brokers

      Fingerprint-linked profiles are often bought and sold by data brokers you've never heard of.

      • In California: the state's Delete Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP) sends one deletion request to every registered data broker at once. It's free.
      • Elsewhere: California's data broker registry lists brokers and how to contact them. You can send them this same letter.
